How Rihla works

🎯

Your taste, through Qloo

The films, books, artists and games you name become Qloo taste signals. Places are ranked by them and by Qloo's Islam and parents with young children audiences — each with an honest reason, like “Because you love Orhan Pamuk”.

🍽️

Halal you can trust

Listed halal, likely halal or ask — never more certainty than the data supports, with a clear “serves alcohol” flag. Bars, pubs and nightclubs are never suggested.

🕌

Built around the prayers

Dhuhr, Asr and Maghrib at the exact time, at the mosque nearest to where you are — and Jumu'ah at a well-known mosque on Fridays.

✅

Checked, not invented

An AI agent researches with tools; then code checks every stop: real places only, opening hours, short walks, meals nearby, nothing repeated.
